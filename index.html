Our Pets — Quick Facts

Dogs

Temperament: Loyal, social, trainable.
Care: Daily exercise, balanced diet, grooming, vaccinations.
Lifespan: 8–15 years (varies by breed).

Cats

Temperament: Independent, curious, affectionate.
Care: Litter box, scratching posts, balanced diet, regular vet checks.
Lifespan: 12–20 years.

Birds

Temperament: Social, vocal (species dependent).
Care: Spacious cage, perches, mental stimulation, varied diet.
Lifespan: 5–80+ years (species dependent).

Fish

Temperament: Quiet, tranquil to active (species dependent).
Care: Proper tank size, filtration, water chemistry, diet.
Lifespan: 1–20+ years (species dependent).

Small Pets

Examples: Rabbits, hamsters, guinea pigs.
Care: Enrichment, clean habitat, appropriate diet, social needs vary.
Lifespan: 2–12 years.
